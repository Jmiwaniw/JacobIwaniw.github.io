MECHANICAL DESIGN / STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS

Rocket transition assembly

Connecting a four-inch avionics section to a six-inch motor and fin-actuation section for a fin-tab rocket.

Role
GNC Structures Subteam Member · Ramblin’ Rocket Club
When
August 2026–Present
Tools / methods
SOLIDWORKS · MATLAB · ANSYS · 3D printing
4″ → 6″Section diameter transition
4Calculation categories
PositiveCalculated margins of safety

The challenge

The transition assembly connects two differently sized rocket sections and transfers loads between them. My work focused on designing the assembly, sizing its load-bearing components, and evaluating structural load paths.

My approach

  1. Design the assembly. Developed and iterated the transition geometry in SOLIDWORKS.
  2. Size the components. Used MATLAB calculations to assess stress, buckling, tear-out, and deflection.
  3. Evaluate load paths. Used ANSYS static structural FEA to investigate simulated stresses.
  4. Refine the design. Updated component geometry, materials, fasteners, and 3D-printed parts based on the analysis.

Results & scope

The calculations produced positive margins of safety across all evaluated components. This case study describes design and analytical evaluation; it does not claim flight qualification or physical load-test results.

What I’m taking forward

This work connects CAD decisions with component-level calculations and assembly-level simulation. It has given me experience moving between those views of the same design and using analysis to guide revisions.
