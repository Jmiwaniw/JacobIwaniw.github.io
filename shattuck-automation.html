The challenge

Manual cell culture verification required more than 20 hours per batch. I developed an image-analysis pipeline to reduce that workload using images from Cell Metric and VIPS imaging systems.

My contribution

I combined a convolutional neural network with a YOLO object-detection model in Python, then trained and validated the model against labeled microscope imagery.

Microscope imageryCNN + YOLOVerification output

Measured results

The pipeline reduced manual verification time to two hours per batch and achieved over 90% accuracy across a 1,000-well dataset. These figures describe the evaluated dataset and workflow.

The operational context

Alongside development, I supported five production runs through sterile material preparation, cell aliquoting, and real-time monitoring and feeding of CHO-cell bioreactors up to 200 liters.
