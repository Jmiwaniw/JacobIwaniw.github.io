The problem

Radiographs can vary in quality. My work focused on developing a classification model and a preprocessing pipeline intended to handle that variability, with low-resource clinical settings in mind.

My contribution

I led model development and engineered the preprocessing and augmentation pipeline to reduce overfitting and improve robustness. I also secured patent-pending status for the core screening process through Duke University’s Office of Translation and Commercialization.

Results & recognition

The model achieved 93.75% accuracy on a dataset of more than 700 images. This is a dataset-level result, not a claim of clinical diagnostic performance.
