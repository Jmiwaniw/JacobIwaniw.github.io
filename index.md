## Selected Work

### Rocket Structures — Ramblin’ Rocket Club

Designed a SOLIDWORKS transition assembly connecting a four-inch
avionics section to a six-inch motor and fin-actuation section.

- Sized load-bearing components using MATLAB stress, buckling,
  tear-out, and deflection calculations.
- Achieved positive calculated margins of safety across all components.
- Evaluated structural load paths using ANSYS static structural FEA.
- Refined geometry, materials, fasteners, and 3D-printed parts based
  on simulated stresses.

**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, MATLAB, ANSYS, 3D printing

### Laboratory Automation — Shattuck Labs

Developed a Python image-analysis pipeline to automate cell culture
monoclonality verification during my technical operations internship.

- Reduced manual verification time from more than 20 hours to
  two hours per batch.
- Trained and validated the model using more than 2,000 labeled
  microscope images.
- Achieved over 90% accuracy across a 1,000-well dataset.

**Tools:** Python, convolutional neural networks, YOLO

### Loops — AR Transcription and Translation Glasses

3D-printed housing for glasses providing live transcription and
translation in more than 25 languages.

Awarded Best Rookie Team at the NC State Hackathon.

[View project](https://devpost.com/software/loops)

osteosarcoma malignancy classification from X-rays.
