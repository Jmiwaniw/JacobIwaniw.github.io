PROTOTYPING / WEARABLE ELECTRONICS

Loops translation glasses

Augmented reality glasses designed for live speech-to-text transcription and translation in more than 25 languages.

Role
Project contributor · Hardware design and integration
When
February 2024
Tools / methods
Arduino · OLED display · Custom 3D-printed housing
25+Languages supported
3D printedCustom housing
Best Rookie TeamNC State Hackathon

The idea

Loops brings live transcription and translation into a wearable display. The project combines software functionality with a physical device that presents information in the user’s line of sight.

My contribution

I designed the glasses and integrated an Arduino microcontroller and OLED display into a custom 3D-printed housing. This work brought together electronics packaging and physical prototyping.

The outcome

The project received the Best Rookie Team award at the NC State Hackathon.

See the project on Devpost

What I learned

The enclosure and electronics need to be considered together. Loops gave me experience turning a software-enabled concept into an integrated physical prototype.
